Our Programmes

What We Do

Our work spans six core programme areas, each designed to create meaningful, lasting impact in the communities we serve.

GBV Prevention & Awareness

We run targeted campaigns and educational workshops to raise awareness about gender-based violence. Our work focuses on prevention through education, addressing root causes, and creating spaces where community members can have open, honest conversations about GBV and its impact.

Youth Outreach

Our youth programmes engage young people through activities, mentorship and skills development. We believe that investing in young people is investing in the future of our communities. Our outreach creates safe, positive environments for youth to grow, learn and lead.

Counselling & Referrals

We provide compassionate, confidential support and connect individuals and families with appropriate professional services. Our referral network helps community members access counselling, healthcare, legal assistance and other essential resources when they need them most.

Community Education

Education is at the heart of empowerment. We deliver workshops, information sessions and community dialogues on topics including GBV, human rights, health and self-empowerment. Our programmes are designed to be accessible, culturally sensitive and impactful.

Awareness Campaigns

Throughout the year we organise and participate in community events, awareness days and campaigns. These bring people together, shine a light on important issues, and celebrate the strength and resilience of our communities, including the annual 16 Days of Activism.

Community & Stakeholder Engagement

We work hand-in-hand with government departments, NGOs, schools, healthcare providers, law enforcement and other community stakeholders. Building these relationships is essential to creating a comprehensive network of support for community members who need help.
