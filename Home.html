Who We Are

A Community-Rooted Non-Profit Organisation

Founded in 2021, Khutsong Empowerment Centre was established to serve and uplift the Khutsong community and surrounding areas in South Africa. We are dedicated to community empowerment, GBV prevention, and ensuring that individuals and families have access to the support and resources they need.

Our team works tirelessly to create safe spaces, run impactful awareness campaigns, and connect people with professional support and referral services.
