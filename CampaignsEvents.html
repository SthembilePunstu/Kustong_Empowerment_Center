Campaigns & Events

From awareness campaigns to community dialogues and youth workshops, our events bring people together to end gender-based violence and build safer communities. Find an event near you and take part.

Featured Campaign

16 Days of Activism Against Gender-Based Violence

Every year from 25 November to 10 December, we join the global call to end violence against women and children. Expect marches, school talks, door-to-door awareness and community dialogues across Khutsong and Carletonville.
