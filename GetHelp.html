Get Help

You Are Not Alone.

If you or someone you know needs support, reaching out is an important first step. Khutsong Empowerment Centre is here to listen, support and guide you — confidentially and with compassion.

Contact Us →

Support Services

Every situation is different. We offer a range of support options so you can find the right help for your circumstances.

Counselling

Access confidential, compassionate counselling support. We can connect you with professional counsellors who understand your situation.

GBV Support

If you are experiencing gender-based violence, you are not alone. We provide immediate support, guidance and a safe, confidential space.

Referrals

We can refer you to appropriate services including shelters, legal aid, healthcare providers and specialised support organisations.

Emergency Assistance

In an emergency situation, your safety is the priority. We can help connect you with emergency services and immediate support.

Community Support

Connect with community support networks, groups and resources to help you through difficult times.
